# Intended Audience

OPOSSUM is designed for users who wish to simulate laser systems of varying scales and complexities.  
These range from large-scale, high-intensity laser facilities to smaller setups, which may function either as independent systems or as subsystems within larger installations.  

A central goal of OPOSSUM is usability: the software aims to lower the entry barrier so that effective simulations can be performed without requiring specialized expertise in simulation software engineering.  
This makes it accessible not only to experienced laser engineers and scientists, but also to newcomers to the field of laser system modeling.  

The primary audience therefore includes laser engineers, researchers, and operators of laser facilities, while the software also welcomes anyone interested in exploring and experimenting with laser simulations.
