# Upcoming Features

OPOSSUM is conceived as a continuously evolving framework. While its present strengths lie in **ray-tracing optical setups**, the long-term vision is to transform it into a comprehensive platform for modeling, analyzing, and optimizing complex laser systems. This development is strongly shaped by community feedback, such as that collected during the OPOSSUM workshop in June 2025, where participants from across Europe shared their priorities and ideas for the future.  

## Towards Version 0.8
The next milestone, **Version 0.8**, will mark the beginning of this expansion. With the planned implementation of **amplification** and **polarization handling**, OPOSSUM will take a decisive step beyond pure ray tracing. These capabilities will allow the modeling of laser gain media and polarization-dependent effects, opening the door to much richer and more realistic simulations.  

## Expanding Horizons
Looking further ahead, OPOSSUM will grow into a versatile environment that, among others, supports:  
- **Automated setup optimization**, enabling users to refine their systems through algorithmic assistance.  
- **Cyclic graphs (resonators)**, allowing the study of feedback loops and cavity dynamics.  
- **Advanced analyzer modes**, including Gaussian mode propagation, Fourier optics, and complex ray-tracing schemes.  

Together, these developments will expand OPOSSUM into a tool that does not merely simulate but actively supports the design of next-generation laser facilities.  

## A Platform for Integration
Beyond new internal capabilities, OPOSSUM is envisioned as a **platform for integration**. Through the addition of **custom nodes**, users will be able to extend the framework with their own models and methods. Rust’s strong interoperability makes it straightforward to connect external codes in, e.g., C/C++, Python, Java etc. This ensures that specialized domain knowledge, whether experimental or computational, can be incorporated seamlessly into a larger simulation.  

These integrations will illustrate how OPOSSUM can act as a bridge between different communities and technologies, fostering collaboration and accelerating scientific progress.  

---

With these developments, OPOSSUM aims not only to remain a powerful ray-tracing engine but to become a **comprehensive ecosystem** for laser-system modeling: modular, extensible, and shaped by its community.
