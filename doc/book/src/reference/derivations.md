# Mathematical derivations

The chapters on concepts describe what OPOSSUM computes in words. Where a result rests on mathematics worth checking, the derivation is collected here: the assumptions it needs, the statements it proves, and what it does not promise.

- [Fluence uncertainty](./derivations/fluence_uncertainty.md): how a fluence detector estimates the deviation of a Voronoi map from the fluence between the rays, and the uncertainty of its peak.
